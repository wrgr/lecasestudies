## Why this file said "queued" for finished work

It was written before the complete-edition pass ran and never updated. Anyone reading it would have
re-executed 167 sites of completed work — the same trap as the stale case numbers in CLAUDE.md's
task list, which pointed a future reviewer at unrelated cases, and the "Gándara JPAM" flag that
would have sent someone to "correct" a correct citation.

just untidiness.** Three instances have now been found in this repo.
