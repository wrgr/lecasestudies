LENS Graduate Attainment Profile

The level a LENS graduate reaches on each ICICLE *Essential Competencies for Learning Engineering Practice* competency

Working document for the ICICLE Competencies, Credentials and Curriculum SIG conversation. Internal draft; Chatham House attribution conventions apply. Not a document of record: docs 1 and 2 win wherever they and this disagree.

# Purpose

The ICICLE *Essential Competencies* framework marks each competency **Individual** (every learning engineer holds it) or **Team** (present somewhere in the effort, not necessarily in one person). LENS makes an *individual* claim: what a graduate can do. These postures are compatible, but the crossing must be explicit, or a reviewer reads a distributed-to-the-team competency as one LENS omits.

This profile states, for each of the 42 competencies, the level a LENS graduate reaches. It is the deliverable called for in `4_LENS_LECF_Crosswalk.md` R2, and it is the companion to `7_LENS_ICICLE_Essential_Competencies_Crosswalk.md`, which establishes coverage; this document sets the *level*. The precedent is IBSTPI (individual competencies validated against practitioners) and INCOSE (proficiency levels per competency); this uses a three-level floor, not the full INCOSE five.

# The three levels

| Level | The graduate… | Where it shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Practitioner** | performs it independently and produces evidence of it | LEN 10 capstone artifacts + a required course |
| **Awareness** | recognizes it and can brief or work with the specialist who holds it (competency 1.b), but does not perform it independently | a course, not the capstone; depth routed to an elective |
| **Out of scope** | does not develop it here; it is held by a teammate or the sibling LXD concentration | stated, not assessed |

**Awareness is not a euphemism for a gap.** It is the framework's own logic: a competency the field marks *Team* can be met by a graduate who can brief its specialist rather than be one. Out of scope is narrower still — competencies a 30-credit concentration deliberately does not build, named so a reviewer does not have to infer them.

# Summary

| Level | Count | Individual competencies (17) | Team competencies (25) |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Practitioner** | 35 | 17 | 18 |
| **Awareness** | 5 | 0 | 5 |
| **Out of scope** | 2 | 0 | 2 |

**Every Individual (floor) competency is at practitioner level.** The five Awareness and two Out-of-scope competencies are all *Team* competencies the framework permits to be distributed. No Individual competency sits below practitioner.

# The Awareness and Out-of-scope competencies, in one place

**Awareness (5):** 1.j develop other practitioners · 1.k build organizational capability · 3.f run a formal study + human-subjects/IRB · 3.h HCD craft · 6.d contribute to the field's evidence base. Each is a *Team* competency; the graduate can brief its specialist and, for 3.f, has a named elective route. None is a floor competency.

**Out of scope (2):** 4.c tools/platform engineering · 4.d content/media production. Both are *Team* competencies whose depth belongs to a different role (software engineer) or a different concentration (LXD). A LENS effort covers them through the team, not the individual — exactly the framework's Team logic.

# How to use this

- **For the SIG.** LENS reaches practitioner on every Individual (floor) competency and on 18 of 25 Team competencies; it names the seven it does not, on the framework's own Individual/Team warrant. That is a proficiency claim the field can check, not a coverage boast.
- **For accreditation.** The Awareness/Out-of-scope rows are the honest answer to "where does research-methods depth / platform engineering / content production live?" — an elective, a teammate, or the sibling concentration, each stated.
- **For a student.** Practitioner rows are what you will be able to do at graduation. Awareness rows are where you consult a specialist — and, for 3.f, the elective to take if you want to hold it yourself.

*Levels are a proposal against the 2018 ICICLE Individual/Team criteria; the criteria are ICICLE's, the level assignments are LENS's and await program-owner adoption alongside docs 1 (v2.4) and 2 (v2.6).*
