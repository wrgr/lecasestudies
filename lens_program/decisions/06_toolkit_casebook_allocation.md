*Draft for review, July 2026. Companion to `LEN01_CLO_MLO_Record.md`. Tag nomenclature follows `lens_program/` docs of record (LEO tier, v2.1/v2.3).*

Purpose: fix which *Learning Engineering Toolkit* chapters and which casebook case types are read in which course, so that no course re-decides and no chapter is either orphaned or read three times.

---

## 1. Chapter 4 is missing from the working list

The chapter inventory circulated for this pass omits **Chapter 4, "Learning Engineering is Engineering"** (Barr, Dargue, Goodell, & Redd). This is the chapter in which the field makes the engineering claim. LEN 1's entire thesis is that the claim is real and that LENS extends it from learning systems to operational systems. Reading Chapters 1, 2, and 3 without Chapter 4 gives students process, learning sciences, and human-centeredness, and withholds the one chapter that argues for the word in the program's name.

Chapter 4 is assigned to LEN 1, Week 1, alongside Chapter 1. If the omission was deliberate, the reason should be recorded, because a reviewer comparing the syllabus to the anchor text's table of contents will notice.

**Also noted:** the Introduction, Chapter 1, and Chapter 2 are open access from Taylor & Francis under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. Week 1 of the concentration therefore requires no purchase, which matters for a first-course drop-risk window.

## 3. Where the Toolkit does not reach

The Toolkit is the field's anchor and LENS builds on it. It does not cover the following, which is why they are LENS's proposed extensions to the ICICLE framework and why each needs supplementary material rather than a chapter assignment.

| LENS competency | Toolkit coverage | Supplement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Capability as the operator-system interface (LEO 1.1) | None. The Toolkit's unit of analysis is the learning system. | Gilbert (1978); Rummler & Brache (1990); LENS working-definition course note |
| Systems lifecycle and HSI (LEO 1.3, 5.2) | None. | INCOSE (2023); Boy & Kennedy (2023); DoD HSI Guidebook (2024) |
| Gap attribution (LEO 4.2) | None. | Gilbert (1978); Rummler & Brache (1990); LENS attribution framework note; casebook |
| Sociotechnical constraint navigation (LEO 5.1) | Partial, via ch. 16. | Woods & Hollnagel (2006); Leveson; Baxter & Sommerville (2011) |
| Human-system collaboration (LEO 3.1–3.5) | None. | Bainbridge (1983); Lee & See (2004); Risko & Gilbert (2016); current literature |
| Coupling failure as empirical anchor | None. | Casebook |

Reading this table across: the Toolkit carries the *process* and the *learning sciences*, and LENS supplies the *system*. That is the program's claim stated as a reading list, which is a useful thing to be able to show a reviewer.

## 4. Casebook usage in LEN 1

**No cases are named yet.** The syllabus assigns a case every week and describes each by what it must teach; identifiers are inserted at LMS build. Fifteen slots, listed in `LEN01_CLO_MLO_Record.md` §4.

Module 5 (Weeks 11–13) carries the three graded case memos. Each memo codes its case against the six-mode failure taxonomy (T/D/N/H/G/K) and names the evidence that would have changed the outcome. The final assessment then takes one case to full analysis and briefing.

**Prerequisites for slot-filling:**
- The six failure modes need written definitions for students. The letters are a coding scheme only once the modes are defined.
- Week 13 needs three cases spanning healthcare, defense, and education at scale, with at least one COI disclosure and one non-peer-reviewed evidence tier among them.
- Week 12 needs a case where a governance objection was raised and the system was withdrawn, so the diagnostic (LEO 1.5) has a real question to answer.

## 5. What this does not settle

- **Case selection for Week 13** (one case per high-consequence domain) needs three cases spanning healthcare, defense, and education at scale. The candidate list should be checked for domain balance; if the defense cases outnumber the others, the sequencing concern for dean-facing materials shows up in a syllabus rather than a memo.
- **The failure-code taxonomy** is referenced in LEN 1 Weeks 11 and 13 and in the final assessment. Doc 1 §Using the Tags names it as the six-mode taxonomy (T/D/N/H/G/K), paired per case with competency tags. LEN 1 needs the mode definitions written out for students; the tag letters alone are not a coding scheme.
- **The LENS working-definition course note, flywheel note, and attribution framework note** are cited as readings and do not yet exist as documents. Three short course notes, each two to four pages.
